Getting there

Both places are in the Allier countryside, in the heart of France. The easiest way to get around is by car, so if you're flying in, we recommend renting one at the airport.

  • From Lyon Saint-Exupéry airport: about 200 km, roughly 2½ hours by car.
  • From the Paris airports: about 315 km from Orly (3½ hours) and 360 km from Charles de Gaulle (4 hours).
  • By train: the nearest station is Dompierre-Sept-Fons, 10 minutes from the château, served by regional trains from Moulins. Moulins-sur-Allier, about 35 minutes away, is the larger station, with direct trains from Paris.

Dress code

Formal. The day begins in church and carries on in the château gardens, so think elegant, but comfortable outdoors: a suit and tie, or a long or cocktail dress.

  • Every colour is welcome, and since this is a garden wedding, we would love to see you in bright, summery shades.
  • Heels are very welcome, but much of the evening is spent on grass: you may want to bring a pair of flat shoes to change into for the garden.
  • August days are warm, but evenings in the countryside can cool down: bring a light layer for later.
